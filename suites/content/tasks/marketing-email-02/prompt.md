# Write a marketing email

Write a useful, respectful product email with a subject, preview text, body, and one call to action. Do not use false urgency or imply unprovided customer data.

Audience: a first-time individual user. Product: a fictional offline notes app. Explain one concrete benefit and a realistic limitation.

Task variant 2: make this response distinct and tailored to the audience and constraints above. Do not claim facts, metrics, customer quotes, or capabilities that were not provided.
