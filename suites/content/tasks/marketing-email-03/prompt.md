# Write a marketing email

Write a useful, respectful product email with a subject, preview text, body, and one call to action. Do not use false urgency or imply unprovided customer data.

Audience: a small nonprofit operations team. Product: a fictional volunteer scheduling tool. Use plain language and avoid promising integrations.

Task variant 3: make this response distinct and tailored to the audience and constraints above. Do not claim facts, metrics, customer quotes, or capabilities that were not provided.
