# Write a marketing email

Write a useful, respectful product email with a subject, preview text, body, and one call to action. Do not use false urgency or imply unprovided customer data.

Audience: a time-constrained team lead. Product: a fictional shared planning tool. Include a useful headline and a clear next step.

Task variant 1: make this response distinct and tailored to the audience and constraints above. Do not claim facts, metrics, customer quotes, or capabilities that were not provided.
